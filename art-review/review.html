Why the styles drifted

Pro's advantage over rd_fast was supposed to be reference_images: feed it the three finished buildings and let it match them. That parameter fails on this account.

Free cost estimates accept reference_images_count: 1 without complaint, so the request shape is legal — it breaks at inference, not validation. Transparency and remove_bg are both ruled out. The upshot: rd_fast has the right style and the wrong projection, Pro has the right projection and the wrong style, and the bridge between them is down.

Also landed: the palette pass

Same dimensions, so the .meta stayed valid and the GUID is unchanged — ClockTowerPrefab still resolves. The visible change is a modest warming rather than a repaint, because the replacement tones were matched to the stone's original luminances to keep its shading structure.

The decision

  • Commit to Pro — accept its look as the style and regenerate the three existing buildings to match ($0.54), buying true isometric across the whole game.
  • Stay on rd_fast — keep the warm brass style and accept front-on projection, as the existing chassis sprites already do. Roughly $0.90 for everything remaining.
  • Debug the reference failure — free to test, since failures aren't billed. If refs can be made to work, both problems close at once.
  • Import this depot first — background-remove and trim it ($0.01), then judge the clash in the actual scene at gameplay zoom.

Balance $4.86 · nothing further spent pending this decision
